LHX: Attack Chopper — GPU 3D, by kznsq
  https://github.com/kznsq/lhx-attack-chopper-gpu
  Revision: 2321564357025483a8e7db4d27e582193b274b38
  License: MIT; see third_party/lhx-gpu-reference.LICENSE
  Original-engine scene layouts, capture/compositing strategy and model
  rendering research informed the native C renderer in runtime/scene_*.c.
  Referenced code includes src/capture.js, src/composite.js, src/reach.js
  and web/gpu.js. The floating-point world traversal and measurements here
  build on that research while preserving the original simulation state.
  Enhanced-mode art, geographic datasets and three.js are not included here.
  Optional face lighting, generated runway textures and flat satellite ground
  adapt web/gpu.js (Relief.shade, Runways.concrete, Terrain) and src/theatre.js.
  Their native implementations are in runtime/scene_renderer.c,
  runtime/scene_texture.c and runtime/scene_assets.c.
  The optional tools/import_terrain.py reads reference images
  into ignored local textures; assets are not redistributed in this repository.
  Horizon haze, static scenery mappings and bounds fitting also adapt
  web/gpu.js (PhotoSky, Props). Optional scenery is prepared locally from the
  reference Kenney Nature, Survival, Industrial and Suburban kits (CC0);
  tools/import_scenery.py retains source hashes and local licence files.
  Native visual relief, terrain mounds, service paint, surface patterns, rotor
  recognition/blades and sun/contact shadows adapt TerrainGrid, hillMounds,
  Relief, militaryPaint, withSurfaceDetail, rotorDiscs, GPUView.blades and
  Props.addShadow in web/gpu.js. Native runway detail adapts Runway.concrete,
  Runway.texture and Runway.add; heading lettering uses local bitmap digits.
  tools/import_relief.py retains reference metadata/data hashes and attribution.
  This project's CPU interpreter is not used.
